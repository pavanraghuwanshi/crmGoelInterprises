SUNDAY POLICY: 
1.    Applicability 
This policy applies to all workers whose standard working schedule falls between Monday to 
Saturday. 
2.    General Rule 
Sunday shall be treated as a paid weekly off, subject to attendance conditions outlined 
below. 
3.   Eligibility Criteria 
A. Full Attendance (6 Days: Monday–Saturday) 
• 
If an employee works all 6 days in a week, they will be: 
o 
Eligible for full paid Sunday of that week. 
B. Partial Attendance (5 Days in a Week) 
A special rolling rule applies: 
• 
If an employee works 5 days in a week: 
o 
They will be eligible for paid Sunday, provided the previous week did NOT 
also have only 5 working days. 
• 
If an employee works 5 days for two consecutive weeks: 
----------------Page (0) Break----------------
o 
Week 1: Sunday → Paid 
o 
Week 2: Sunday → Not Paid 
• 
If the employee continues working 5 days in Week 3: 
o 
Week 3 Sunday → Paid again 
      In simple terms: 
• 
Paid Sunday is given for alternate weeks when attendance is consistently 5 days. 
 
5. Summary Table 
Week        Days Worked    Sunday Paid? 
Week 1    6 days                     Yes 
Week 1    5 days                     Yes 
Week 2    5 days                    No 
Week 3    5 days                     Yes 
Week 4    5 days                    No 
 
 
OVERTIME POLICY (THRESHOLD-BASED) 
1. Objective 
To define overtime eligibility based on actual working time beyond scheduled duty hours, 
with a minimum threshold requirement. 
2. Overtime Eligibility Rule 
A. Minimum Threshold Condition 
• 
Overtime will only be applicable if extra working time exceeds 30 minutes. 
• 
Any extra time up to 30 minutes (≤30 mins) will not be considered for overtime. 
3. Overtime Calculation 
A. Post Shift (After Off Time) 
• 
If an employee stays beyond their scheduled off time: 
----------------Page (1) Break----------------
Extra Time Worked      Overtime Eligibility 
Up to 30 minutes             Not Paid 
More than 30 minutes     Full extra time paid 
Example: 
• 
Off time: 7:00 PM 
o 
Leaves at 7:20 PM →    No overtime 
o 
Leaves at 7:31 PM →     31 minutes overtime paid 
 
B. Pre Shift (Before In Time) 
• 
If an employee starts work before their scheduled in time: 
Early Time                                Overtime Eligibility 
Up to 30 minutes early             Not Paid 
More than 30 minutes early     Full extra time paid 
Example: 
• 
In time: 9:00 AM 
o 
Arrives at 8:40 AM →    No overtime 
o 
Arrives at 8:29 AM →     31 minutes overtime paid 
 
4. Important Conditions 
• 
This kind of Overtime must be approved by management/supervisor. 
• 
Unauthorized early/late presence will not qualify for overtime. 
 
5. Key Principle 
      Overtime starts only after crossing the 30-minute threshold, and once crossed, entire 
extra duration is counted.
